# Conclusion

THEKERNEL_SYSTEM_TEST_COMPLETE is a shutdown trigger, not a pass verdict. A gate pass requires the complete KTAP log to contain no FAIL or SKIP and requires QEMU to exit through the normal guest-shutdown path.

# Evidence

At TheKernel commit 84464080410c076f62f9496b86fa22f8a2ac05be, tests/guest/system-init.c lines 678–702 show that the guest emits the marker after zero failures even when skips are nonzero. tools/thekernel.py `reject_ktap_skips_in_log`, lines 424–432, supplies the separate zero-SKIP check, and `run_product`, lines 419–421, applies it only after `guest_clean_shutdown`. tests/test_thekernel_system_test.py `test_ktap_skip_is_rejected_by_default_gate`, lines 50–59, verifies that rejection.
